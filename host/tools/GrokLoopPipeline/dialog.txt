MARY: Lamb, stay close — the schoolyard is loud today.
LAMB: (soft bleat) Mary…
MARY: Misses said no animals past the gate, but you followed the whole way.
TEACHER: Mary, is that your lamb again?
MARY: Yes, ma'am. He won't sleep unless he hears my voice.
TEACHER: Then let him sit by the window. One afternoon. No chewing the primers.
MARY: Thank you. Lamb, quiet as a cloud.
LAMB: (settles) …
NARRATOR: And so the lamb waited through sums and songs, until the bell and Mary's hand brought him home again under the evening sky.
