HOUSE RULES:
- Dialogue: audible speech + visible lip motion for the speaker; never silent mouths on dialogue.
- Silent/ambient: no open-mouth shout, lip-sync, or speech-performance; restrained tension (tight jaw, fist, held stare) is OK.
- Continuity: when extending from the previous clip, match wardrobe, place, and facing from the last frames.
- Honor VOICE LOCK, character visual locks, and any CAST COUNT line exactly — no extra faces, crowds, or role swaps.
- Performance: facial expression and gaze match the beat and project PERFORMANCE rules (confessional address vs objective). On dialogue, mouth and energy match the line; do not invent rage on a calm line. Keep eyes readable unless sleep/death/covered is planned. No beauty-blank or wrong-emotion default face.
- Style: match locked character refs and project medium (picture-book CG, photoreal, etc.). Do not drift medium mid-film or merge Character_* faces.
- Prefer tight action after speech; avoid long empty holds.
